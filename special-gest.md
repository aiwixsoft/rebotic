## Muhammad Sohail Khan Afridi
Title: Chief Minister of KP
Background: A grassroots political worker and first-time MPA from PK-70 Khyber-II, he formerly served as the provincial president of the Insaf Students Federation (ISF). He was chosen by PTI leadership to succeed Ali Amin Gandapur.
Contributions: Focused heavily on water management and agricultural security, he actively pushes to accelerate mega-infrastructure projects including the Chashma Right Bank Canal Lift Canal, Mohmand Dam, and the Dasu Hydropower Project. His administration prioritizes anti-corruption measures in cabinet governance, rural development, and expanding communication infrastructure.

## Riaz Khan
Title: Provincial Irrigation Minister
Background: Appointed as a key provincial minister within the expanded KP cabinet.
Contributions: Spearheading the land acquisition and implementation phases of vital agricultural irrigation schemes. He works alongside the CM to oversee water resource developments designed to bring over 295,000 acres of barren land under active cultivation.

## Muzammil Aslam
Title: Adviser to the Chief Minister on Finance
Background: Serving as the chief financial strategist and adviser for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa provincial government.
Contributions: Managing the provincial budget, navigating fiscal allocations for massive hydel projects, and driving financial policies aimed at stabilizing the economic environment of KP amid national financial shifts.

## Meena Khan
Title: Minister for Local Government & Higher Education
Background: Holds dual portfolios within the provincial cabinet, overseeing grassroots governance and academic infrastructure.
Contributions: Driving reforms in municipal elections, upgrading rural infrastructure, and spearheading capacity-building programs across higher education archives and libraries to increase youth literacy and employment.

## Faisal Karim Kundi
Title: Governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Background: Serving as the federally appointed constitutional head of the province.
Contributions: Overseeing the constitutional operations of the KP Assembly, mediating legal and administrative friction between provincial lawmakers and the federal government, and executing the formal approvals of legislative acts and cabinet appointments.
